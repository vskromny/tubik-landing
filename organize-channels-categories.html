Organize your child's YouTube channels into categories

A long list of channel names is hard for a young child. In Tubik you sort the channels you approve into categories you name, such as Music or Nature, and your child sees one large tile each. Tubik is an independent app and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.

Name your own categories

Create a category, give it a name and choose a picture, then move channels into it. You can rename or remove categories whenever you like.

A simple shelf for your child

On the child side each category is a large picture tile. Your child taps a tile to see videos from the channels in that category, without a list of channel names to read.

A time budget per category

Each category can have its own daily budget. When a category's time is used up, its tile stays closed for the rest of the day while other categories stay available within the daily total.

What to keep in mind

Videos play in YouTube's official embedded player, which can show its own ads or overlays. Categories sort what you have approved; they do not rate the content of a channel, so look at a channel before you add it.

Common questions

Can I change categories later?

Yes. You can rename categories, move channels between them and remove them in parent mode.

Is Tubik affiliated with YouTube?

No. Tubik is an independent app by a single developer and is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
