How to set a daily video time limit for your child

Set a daily total and a budget per category. Tubik helps keep videos within the budget and shows a goodbye screen when time is up. It counts time inside Tubik only.

A daily total and a budget per category

Each child profile has a daily total. Each category, for example “Music” or “Nature”, can have its own daily budget on top. While your child watches a category, the smaller of the two applies. When a category's time is used up, its tile rests until tomorrow.

The end is gentle

In the last minutes of the budget Tubik slowly lowers the screen brightness as a quiet signal that the end is coming. You can change the number of minutes or switch it off per profile. If time runs out while a video is nearly over, Tubik lets it finish within a short grace period; otherwise it moves to a goodbye screen.

“Stop after first video”

In parent mode you can switch on “Stop after first video” before you start a session, or turn on stop-after for a session that is already running. The session then ends when the current video finishes. It is useful when you want one video to be the last.

What the timer does not do

The timer covers the time spent inside Tubik. It does not limit other apps. For limits across the whole device, use Apple's Screen Time in iOS Settings; the two can be used together.

Common questions

Does Tubik need an account?

No. There is no Tubik account and you do not sign in to YouTube. Time budgets are stored on the device.

Where do the videos come from?

From YouTube channels you approve. They play in YouTube's own embedded player, which has its own data handling and may show its own ads on some videos.
