Choose which YouTube channels your child can watch

Tubik is an iPhone and iPad app for parents of young children. Instead of an open feed, your child sees a shelf built from the channels you chose.

You build the list

In parent mode, open Channels and tap Add. Paste a link to a channel or to any of its videos. Searching by name is also possible but limited to a few searches a day, because YouTube limits how often an app may ask. You sort the channels into categories you name yourself.

What your child sees

A calm shelf of large picture tiles, one per category. The child side has no search field and no comment section, and Tubik adds no recommendations of its own. It shows videos from the channels in your categories, minus the ones you have hidden. When a video ends, Tubik moves to its own screen instead of the end-of-video suggestions. YouTube's player may still show its own overlays or ads.

You stay in control

Parent mode sits behind a PIN. You can hide a single video, remove a channel, and set time budgets per category. If you forget the PIN, there is no account to recover it from: you reset the app on that device.

What to keep in mind

Videos play in YouTube's official embedded player, unmodified. YouTube may show its own ads inside the player on some videos, and Tubik cannot change that. Channels can change what they publish, so it is worth looking at a channel before you add it.

Common questions

Is Tubik made by YouTube?

No. Tubik is an independent app by a single developer. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by YouTube or Google.

What does it cost?

Tubik is free to download, with no in-app purchases in this version.
