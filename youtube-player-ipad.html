A parent-controlled YouTube player for your child on iPad

Tubik works on iPad and iPhone, with the same approved channels, categories and time budgets and a layout made for the larger screen. Tubik is an independent app and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.

Made for the larger screen

On iPad your child sees rows of videos grouped by category. Parent mode uses the extra space for a sidebar, and Tubik works in portrait and landscape.

The same parent controls

You approve channels, sort them into categories and set a daily total and a budget per category, the same as on iPhone. Parent mode sits behind a PIN.

What to keep in mind

Videos play in YouTube's official embedded player, which can show its own ads or overlays. Tubik limits time spent inside Tubik; for limits across the whole iPad, use Apple's Screen Time in Settings.

Common questions

Do I need a separate iPad app?

No. Tubik is one app for iPhone and iPad.

Does it work in landscape?

Yes. Tubik works in portrait and landscape on iPad.
